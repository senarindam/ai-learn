Simple definitions

The AI family tree

AI is the broader goal. Models are the engines. Agents are the workers that plan, use tools, and coordinate models to complete tasks.

01

Artificial Intelligence

Systems that sense, reason, predict, create, or act in ways we call intelligent.

02

Large Language Models

Broad, powerful language models trained on massive text and code collections.

03

Small Language Models

Compact models optimized for speed, privacy, cost, and focused domain tasks.

04

AI Agents

Goal-driven systems that use models, memory, tools, and feedback loops.

How they talk

From question to useful result

A practical AI system is usually a conversation between people, models, tools, data, and feedback.

Human goal

"Plan my study timetable" or "Analyze our customer churn."

Agent planner

Breaks the goal into steps, asks clarifying questions, and chooses tools.

LLM + SLM team

The LLM reasons broadly; SLMs handle faster, focused, private tasks.

Tools and feedback

Search, databases, calculators, documents, and people improve the answer.

How AI is evolving

From prediction to action

The big shift is from AI that only answers questions to AI that can help plan, use tools, remember context, and safely execute work.

Past

Rules and classic ML

Systems followed explicit rules or learned narrow patterns, such as spam detection, recommendations, and forecasting.

Now

LLMs and SLMs

Large models handle broad reasoning and language. Small models bring focused, faster, cheaper intelligence into products.

Next

Agents and teams of models

Agents coordinate models, tools, memory, permissions, and human approvals to complete real workflows responsibly.
